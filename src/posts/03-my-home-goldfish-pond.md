Let me present you something aesthetic, it is my home's goldfish pond, the pond contains several kind of fishes such as goldfish, zebra fish, tetra fish, guppies, and also several kind of water plants, but the highlight are these cutie goldfish. 

![The big eyes goldfish](/assets/images/big-eyes-goldfih.png "The big eyes goldfish")

The **big eyes goldfish** is considered as the prettiest goldfish in the pond, at first when it was a child, there is no much color on the skin, combining with the weird large eyes, making it looks ugly. But when it grows up, the color appears a lot, and it somehow makes its appearance look so much prettier, much like the story of *The Ugly Duckling*

![The fury goldfish](/assets/images/fury-goldfih.png "The fury goldfish")

The **fury goldfish** is bought from the market at a higher price because it already has 3 colors appearing on its skin. It is the furiest goldfish in the pond, swimming very fast and barely staying still. Making a snap with it is pretty much difficult.

![The creamy goldfish](/assets/images/creamy-goldfih.png "The creamy goldfish")

The **creamy goldfish** is the least attractive-looking goldfish in the pond. Despite its look, it is the toughest one in the pond. There were a couple of times when this fish was nearly dead for some reason, but it somehow survived.

![Goldfish duo, the big eyes and the fury](/assets/images/duo-goldfih.png "Goldfish duo, the big eyes and the fury")

There are still two goldfish that I didn't take. They are all bubble head oranda goldfish, and they could be the highlight of this pond due to their cute bubble head and gorgeous fin and tail. Unfortunately, one is quite old, and it always stay at the bottom of the pond, so I couldn't take a snap of it. The other one is sick and is in quarantine for treatment.

**Update:** The one in quarantine died last night from the severe rain. Rest in peace, my little friend.

I shoot these pictures by my iPhone 11 and done some editing with [RawTherapee](https://rawtherapee.com/). I wish I could have a polarizing filter for the camera lens, because the water reflection got me irritated. Also I wish I could have some nice rugged camera just to dip into the pond and shoot the picture in the different underwater perspective.
